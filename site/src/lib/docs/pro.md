ctail is free to use and open source. **ctail Pro** is a one-time in-app purchase on the Mac App Store that unlocks the power features. There is no subscription and no account; the purchase is tied to your Apple ID and works on every Mac you sign in to.

## Buying

Choose **ctail ▸ Unlock ctail Pro…**, or simply try a Pro feature, and the Pro window opens with the price in your local currency. Purchases go through Apple, so ctail never sees your payment details.

## Restoring

Reinstalled, or set up a new Mac? Choose **ctail ▸ Restore Purchases**. Pro is restored from your Apple ID.

## TestFlight

In TestFlight builds the App Store sandbox is used, so the Pro purchase is free to try and is not charged.

## Source builds

If you build ctail from the GitHub repository there is no App Store licensing. Debug builds have an **Unlock Pro (dev)** item in the ctail menu that enables the Pro features locally. Release builds made outside the App Store do not include StoreKit, so consider buying the App Store version if you find ctail useful.
